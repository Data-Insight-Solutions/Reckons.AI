# Content Safety

> **Production** — built, tested, and in use.

An ethics preamble is added to the prompts where someone other than you may read the result: always when sharing, and for conversation with a remote model. Private local use and prompts whose every written statement is already checked by the classifier go without it. Content classifier with two levels: blocked (filtered out on ingest) and mature (flagged on export with advisory). Discourse, disagreement, and academic content pass freely.

<p class="derived">This is built, working, and in daily use here.</p>
