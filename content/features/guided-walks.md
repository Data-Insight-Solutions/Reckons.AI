# Guided walks: a story you step through

> **Functional** — built and working, with rough edges still being smoothed.

A story is a short guided tour stored inside a collection. Open one with /?story (the collection's own story) or /?story=&lt;the story's address&gt;, and Shelly plays it. A walk shows which step you are on (step N of M), Next and Back work from the keyboard and by touch, and each step lights the entities it is about and dims the rest without moving anything. A step can also offer a button: open a page in the app, or change one setting. You always press the button yourself; a story never changes a setting on its own, and only three settings can be offered (Triple Check on or off, its web half off only, and 2D or 3D). Nothing that sends data anywhere, such as an address, a key or a model, can be offered. The Data Insight Solutions example comes with a generated story, five or six steps in plain words for a first visitor, whose counts (pages, posts, facts) are worked out from the build, not typed.

<p class="derived">This is built and working.</p>

## Why it is this way

**Honest Note**

EARLY. Only the Triple Check walkthrough uses the buttons so far; the other example stories do not. A step cannot yet spotlight a whole group of entities or open an image, clip or splat full screen. The new step counter and the link that reopens a step are described in the roadmap; the browser test for them is not shown as passing in this release's notes.
