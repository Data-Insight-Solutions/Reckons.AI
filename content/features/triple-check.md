# Triple Check

Optional continuous checking of statements against cited web evidence and all spaces on your device.

- Each check retains dated citations. A likely true or false label summarizes a small sample of distinct publisher passages; it is not proof or a calibrated probability. Copied pages do not establish independence. You may ignore suggestions, mark evidence reviewed, or decide whether to refine a statement. Checking never accepts or rewrites your assertions automatically.
- Open Settings → Triple Check to choose search, page scraping and evidence-model integrations. Enable checking for this space. The app checks pending and retained assertions while open, deduplicates repeated claims, and shows possible conflicts on statement cards and in Review. All local spaces are compared, excluding archives; reference spaces can supply product terminology. Optional meaning comparison uses your Ollama endpoint. Web checking can be disabled for sensitive content.
- Repository comparison views show a selected shortlist, dated GitHub metrics and cited qualitative proposals. Save selected entities, relationships or specific assertions as a space view. The canvas limits visible entities by default and discloses hidden statements; expanding the view changes only the display. Analyze can explicitly apply purpose-based organization with a recovery snapshot. The bundled synthetic research space demonstrates conflicting document corpora.

## Why it is this way

**Honest Note**

Evidence is stored in this browser and is not currently exported with the space. Paid provider accuracy and cost have not been calibrated. Enterprise SME discovery, RBAC and approved evidence projections remain planned; local checking provides no enterprise access enforcement.
