# Triple Check

> **In progress** — actively being built. Parts of what follows may not work yet.

Optional continuous checking of statements against your other spaces and, if you allow it, cited web pages. What it finds arrives in Review as pending statements from a new source.

<p class="derived">This is being built now.</p>

- Each check retains dated citations. A likely true or false label summarizes a small sample of distinct publisher passages; it is not proof or a calibrated probability. Copied pages do not establish independence. You may ignore suggestions, mark evidence reviewed, or decide whether to refine a statement. Checking never accepts or rewrites your assertions automatically.
- Open Settings → Triple Check and enable checking for this space. While the app is open it checks one statement about once a minute. A page or another space that says the same thing becomes the same statement from that source, which you can accept as an extra citation. One that says something different becomes its own statement beside yours. A page that disputes a statement without saying what is true instead becomes a question. Nothing is accepted for you, and a proposal you rejected is not made again. In Review, group by source to accept or reject everything one source added at once; statements that disagree are held back for their own decision. All local spaces are compared, excluding archives. Optional meaning comparison uses your Ollama endpoint. Web checking sends each checked statement to the search service and model you chose, and can be turned off for sensitive content. To try it, open the walkthrough from Settings → Triple Check.
- Repository comparison views show a selected shortlist, dated GitHub metrics and cited qualitative proposals. Save selected entities, relationships or specific assertions as a space view. The canvas limits visible entities by default and discloses hidden statements; expanding the view changes only the display. Analyze can explicitly apply purpose-based organization with a recovery snapshot. The bundled synthetic research space demonstrates conflicting document corpora.

## Why it is this way

**Honest Note**

Evidence is stored in this browser and is not currently exported with the space. Paid provider accuracy and cost have not been calibrated. Enterprise SME discovery, RBAC and approved evidence projections remain planned; local checking provides no enterprise access enforcement.
