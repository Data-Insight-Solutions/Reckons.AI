# Proposals: decisions that cite their evidence

> **Planned** — on the roadmap, **not yet built**. Described here as intended, not as shipped.

A proposal is how Reckons.AI will put a decision in front of you: two to four options, each listing the facts it rests on, and through those facts the passages they came from, so you can follow any option back to its evidence before you choose. The shape of a proposal is now defined and checked: what a valid one must contain, the reasons an invalid one is refused, and the stages it moves through from suggested to decided.

<p class="derived">This is planned and not built yet. It has one part below.</p>

## Why it is this way

**Honest Note**

A DEFINITION ONLY. Nothing in the app makes, stores or shows a proposal yet. Building the options with their citations, and the screen to review them, are planned.

## In this section

**[How held facts, proposals and questions look on the canvas](../features/standing-on-canvas)**

Every fact has a standing, worked out from fields it already has: HELD (you confirmed it), PENDING (waiting for your review), PROPOSED (waiting, and part of one option of a proposal) or QUESTION (its object is not known yet).
