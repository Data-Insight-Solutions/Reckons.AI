# Passage Grounding

> **Production** — built, tested, and in use.

Verbatim source excerpts attached to extracted triples. LLM prompt rule requests the exact source sentence. Persists via meta:excerpt in TTL reification. Displayed in StatementCard and DiffEntry.

<p class="derived">This is built, working, and in daily use here. It has 2 parts below.</p>

<p class="in-sets">Part of Work out the claims.</p>

## In this section

**[Open a fact's source at the exact words](../features/open-in-source)**

When Reckons.AI reads a source and proposes a fact, the fact records exactly which stretch of text it came from: the passage, where the quote starts and ends, and a fingerprint of the source's text at that moment.

**[Where a node's facts came from](../features/node-sources)**

Select a node and its details now show which sources its facts came from.
