# Where a node's facts came from

Select a node and its details now show which sources its facts came from. Under each source are the facts that source gave, so you can see at a glance what came from where. Click a source's title to open it in Sources, where it is selected for you and any filter that would hide it is cleared. Facts are listed whichever direction they point, even when the Statements filters would hide them.
