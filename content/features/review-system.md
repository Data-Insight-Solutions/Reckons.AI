# Review — where your graph gets smaller and truer

The step where you decide what is true. A model proposes triples; you confirm, refine or reject each one, and nothing enters your graph without that. It is the slowest part of the product and the only reason the rest of it is worth anything.

<p class="derived">It has one part below.</p>

<p class="in-sets">Part of You settle it.</p>

## At a glance

**Audience**

Anyone weighing this against a notes app, and wondering why they should do the extra work.

## Why it is this way

**Principle**

- THE POINT IS NOT GATEKEEPING. IT IS SUPERSEDING. A notepad accumulates: write we are going with Aprimo in March and we chose Bynder in July, and both sentences sit there forever with nothing to reconcile them. Search finds whichever matched your words. A retrieval system indexes both and will happily cite the March one at you in September, with a confident tone and a real quotation. Review is the moment a new fact REPLACES an old one instead of joining it — the superseded statement keeps its place in the record, marked as superseded, and stops being what your graph says.
- THE QUEUE SHOWS WHAT CHANGED, NOT EVERYTHING FOUND. New claims, near-duplicates that are probably one thing under two names, and — the valuable ones — claims that CONTRADICT something you already accepted. A contradiction is the moment a pile of documents would have stayed quiet. Finding it is what you are paying the review time for.
- WHICH MEANS THE GRAPH GETS SMALLER AND TRUER OVER TIME. That is the property no append-only store has, and it is the whole return on the work. A year of notes is a year of sediment you must re-read. A year of reviewed facts is a year of decisions you can act on, with the discarded ones still visible if you want to know what you used to think.

**Constraint**

IT IS GENUINELY SLOW AT THE START, and this page will not pretend otherwise. A graph of nine facts answers nothing worth asking, and getting past that costs you review time before it returns anything. If you want a tool that reads your files and answers confidently without asking, that exists and this is not it.
