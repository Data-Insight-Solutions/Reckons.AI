# Getting started arrives already reviewed

> **Functional** — built and working, with rough edges still being smoothed.

The Getting started space is a small made-up story: two friends, Alex and Jordan, planning a camping weekend. It comes with seven short sample sources (a text thread, a voice memo, notes on each campground, a forecast and route notes), each opening with a line that says it is a sample. Its 48 facts each show the passage they came from, who accepted them and when, including two corrections and two rejections, so the review history you see is a real one. Three facts are left for you: the Review tab opens with them waiting for your decision.
