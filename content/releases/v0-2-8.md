# Reckons.AI v0.2.8

This is the **website builder release**. Most of it is about one idea: a website can be built
from the same kind of knowledge you keep in Reckons.AI, and the website itself can then show
how it was made. It also adds a way to walk through a story one step at a time, and it begins
work on turning photographs of a real object into a 3D asset.

Be clear about one thing first: parts of this release are finished and parts are not. Each item
below says which. Where something is only partly done, it says what is missing.

## Build a website from a folder of pages

- **One command builds the site.** A folder of pages becomes a finished group of web pages. You can
  run it yourself on your own computer.
- **A blog.** An index page, posts, categories, cover pictures and a short note on what a
  post cites. Drafts stay out of the finished site unless you ask to preview them.
- **A landing page you can reuse.** A front page with a soft moving background, cards, a contact
  form that posts to your own address, light and dark looks, and a menu that works on a phone. Colors,
  fonts and the contact address come from the site's own settings, not from the code.
- **Balanced layouts and a check for them.** Sections lay out in even grids with no empty gap on
  one side, a principles block can show as cards, and the scroll control works on sections pages.
  A check measures every row of a built page for an empty column and reports it. The check is
  new and has been run on the Data Insight Solutions About page.
- **Pages with sections.** A page can hold blocks of text, small diagrams, logos and small print,
  and an optional picture or video. Linked files can show when they were last changed.
- **A guided scroll.** An optional control on the front page that walks a visitor down the page
  section by section.
- **Files for search engines.** When you give the site its address, the build writes a robots file
  and a sitemap and marks each page's preferred address. It never makes up an address.

## A website that shows how it was made

- **The Data Insight Solutions site as an example space.** You can open a real company website as
  a space inside Reckons.AI, from a card on the About page: its pages, the references behind them,
  and the facts that several pages state.
- **The story of how it was made.** The site can carry a short story told
  in plain words, with counts taken from the actual build, and it plays through the guided walk
  below. It has been looked at in Chromium only, and it is not live until this release deploys.

## Guided walks

- **Step N of M.** A story can now be walked one step at a time with Next and Back, the arrow
  keys, or a bar of steps on a phone. Each step lights the parts of the picture it talks about and
  dims the rest, without moving anything. A link can open a space at a chosen step.

## Facts that expire

- **A date on a fact.** A fact, such as a certification, can carry a "valid until" date. A check
  lists every fact that is past its date or close to it, and names every page that states it. A
  site build refuses to publish a page that states an expired fact, and says which one.
- **On the real site.** The Data Insight Solutions About page now states one dated fact, a
  certification valid to December 2027, and the check reports it as in date. The check runs as a
  script on request; it does not yet block a release by itself.

## A ring, scanned into a 3D asset (in progress)

- **From photographs to a 3D object.** A ring was filmed on a scan board and rebuilt into a 3D
  picture you can orbit. It is reconstructed from the photographs, not drawn by an AI model, and
  every label says so. It has its own public page of the whole story, opened by a link
  rather than listed with the examples.
- **The ring page, now in ten steps (in progress).** Besides the 16 second video clip and a few
  of its frames, the page walks through every step from filming to the orbitable object, then two
  more. Step 9 is an honest result: a script that picks sharp frames by itself kept 199 of 1,595
  candidates, and the second 3D picture was not clearly sharper than the first. Step 10 says what
  comes next: filming several videos of two rings, to be combined into one 3D picture. The 3D picture on the page is still the first one.
- **Frame picking by script (early).** A command that scores video frames by how sharp the object
  is, keeps the best in each stretch of the orbit and reports gaps. It is built and tested on small
  made-up images, and it has been run once on the ring video. It is marked early, not finished,
  because it did not clearly beat picking the frames by hand.
- **Several videos, one 3D picture (early).** A command that takes two or more videos of the same
  object, keeps the sharp frames from each, works out where the camera was in all of them at once, and
  trains one 3D picture from the lot. It refuses to continue, and names the video, if one of them
  cannot be placed with the others. It has run once, on one video cut into two halves; it has not
  yet been tried on separately filmed videos, so it is marked early.
- **Scan from hand-picked frames (in progress).** Instead of letting the computer choose which
  video frames to use, you can choose them yourself: a folder of sharp pictures from different
  angles. The code that reads such a folder is built and tested on small made-up images. A full
  scan run from a hand-picked folder has not been shown yet.
- **An embeddable viewer (in progress).** A small viewer that a website can show the 3D asset
  in: it turns slowly, you drag to rotate it, and it stays on the site's own address. It is large
  to download, about one megabyte compressed.
- **A finished scan joins your space (early).** When a scan finishes, its 3D picture is added to
  your work for you to review. It is built but has only been tested in pieces.

## Seeing what is a proposal

- **A new value that may disagree with what you have is held back for you to choose.** When it is
  not yet known whether something takes one value or several, a different value waits, showing
  what you already have, and you pick replace, keep both or reject. Nothing is replaced silently.
  The exception is a newer value from the same origin, such as 20 tests passing becoming 22: that
  is recorded as an update, with the old value kept as history, and you are not asked.

- **Facts show how settled they are.** A fact is now held (you confirmed it), waiting for review,
  part of a proposal, or a question. The picture draws these differently: proposals are dashed and
  questions are hollow, so you can tell them apart even in a grey printout.
- **Filters for proposals and questions (in progress).** Built, but not yet fully checked.

## Better review for the agents that help build this

- **Every proposal says what it will do.** A row in the review queue now names its space, the run
  that made it and whether accepting it writes a fact, answers a question or only reports a
  finding.
- **Checks agents cannot talk their way past.** A report from an agent has to carry evidence that
  a script can read again; nothing private may leave your machine in a branch; and watching a job
  costs less than the job.
- **Checks on generated pictures, documents and 3D files.** A generated file is checked,
  reviewed by a vision model, and fixed again within a limit, keeping the best attempt.
- **Gaps between where you are and where you want to be.** The Reckoning can now work out which
  requirements of a target are unmet. This is a calculation only; no screen shows it yet.
- **Smaller improvements.** A 3D picture can be attached to any item and loads only when it is on
  screen. A job that would push a model out of your graphics card into slower memory is stopped.
  When Ollama replaces the model you chose, you now see a notice. A This Device record of every
  local job exists as a script, but the page it feeds is not built yet.
- **Triple Check (in progress).** Checking a claim against the open web and your other
  collections, as proposals only. It is switched on by you, runs on your device, and has not been
  measured for accuracy against live results.

## What is not finished

- The ring page, hand-picked frames, the embeddable
  viewer, scan attaching, Triple Check and the filters above are each described as in progress or
  early, because they are.
- The editor for the website (a content manager) and publishing a built site to the web are not
  in this release.

## Moved to v0.2.9

v0.2.8 was first planned as other things, and then as an assets-and-demos release built around a
demo video. None of that was working, so it moves to **v0.2.9** rather than shipping half
built: the demo video pipeline, reckoning a generated asset, Open in other apps, better generated
video, scanning one place from several captures, serving Reckons.AI from your own computer to your
devices, time forecasting, and the canvas and analysis work. Moved because it was not ready, not
because it was dropped.
