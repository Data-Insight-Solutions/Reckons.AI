# Welcome to Reckons.AI

Reckons.AI is a place to keep what you know so that you can ask it questions
later. You add things — a note, a page you read, something you said out loud —
and instead of storing the words, it works out the **claims** inside them and
asks you to confirm each one. What you end up with is not a pile of documents.
It is a set of facts you have personally agreed to, each one still attached to
where it came from.

That extra step in the middle is the whole product, and it is the part worth
understanding before anything else. It is also the part that is easy to mistake
for busywork, so there is a page about exactly that: [why a graph, and not a
notepad](../use-cases/why-not-a-notepad).

## What it is good for

- **Deciding something** where the reasons matter and you will be asked to
  justify them later — an insurance dispute, a vendor choice, a diagnosis.
- **Comparing two accounts** of the same thing and seeing precisely where they
  disagree, rather than reading both again.
- **Keeping a year of your own notes usable**, when you no longer remember how
  you phrased something the first time.

## What it is not

It is not a notes app with extra steps, and it is not an assistant that reads
your files and answers confidently. Nothing enters your graph because a model
suggested it. Every fact was proposed, shown to you, and accepted — which is why
the graph can be trusted later, and why it can tell you what it does **not**
know.

## About this site

These pages are generated from the same kind of graph you would build yourself.
Every heading, status badge and diagram here is a fact somewhere in a `.ttl`
file, and the site is rebuilt from it — so the documentation cannot quietly
disagree with the thing it documents. When a page says a feature is
`scaffolded`, that word came from the graph, not from a writer's memory.
