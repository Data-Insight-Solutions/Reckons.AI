# Who thought of it — people in this story

A short list, and deliberately short: these are the people whose specific ideas this product rests on, not a hall of fame. Each entry says what they did that Reckons.AI actually uses.

## In this section

### Dan Brickley

Co-creator of FOAF (Friend of a Friend) vocabulary and Schema.org. Demonstrated that RDF could describe social networks long before Facebook.

### Matthew Roe

Owner-operator of Data Insight Solutions LLC. Creator of Reckons.AI. Designed the local-first architecture, human-in-the-loop review workflow, and the graph Leap cross-referencing system.

Founder & Developer

### Pat Hayes

Logician and AI researcher. Co-author of the RDF Semantics specification. His work on formal semantics gave RDF its rigorous logical foundation.

### Tim Berners-Lee

Inventor of the World Wide Web (1989) and the Semantic Web vision (2001). Founded W3C. Proposed the Linked Data principles: use URIs, use HTTP, provide RDF, link to other datasets.
