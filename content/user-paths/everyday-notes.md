# Keeping everyday notes

*Concept*

> **Scaffolded** — the structure exists, but it is incomplete. Expect gaps.

You want to write things down the way you always have — loosely, in your own words, without deciding up front what any of it means. The difference here is what happens next: the note is kept as you wrote it AND read for the separate claims inside it, so later you can ask what you actually know rather than search for a paragraph you half remember.

## Steps

**[Write it the way you would have anyway](../user-paths/everyday-1)**

No template, no fields, no deciding what kind of note this is.

**[Let it find the claims you made without noticing](../user-paths/everyday-2)**

A sentence usually asserts more than one thing.

**[Correct it, because it will be wrong sometimes](../user-paths/everyday-3)**

Extraction is measured, not assumed: the best model tested recovers about three quarters of what a careful reader would.

## Details

**Audience**

- Anyone who takes notes. The most common way in, and the least well served today.

**Ends With**

- The note kept verbatim, plus the claims inside it settled as facts you can query and connect.

**Honest Note**

- READ THE STATUS. You can write a freeform note today at /ingest and it will be extracted into reviewable facts — that part works. What does NOT exist is a note-taking SURFACE: no notebook, no daily page, no editing a note after you wrote it, no browsing your notes as notes rather than as graph nodes. Today a note is an input to extraction and then it is a source record. If you arrived wanting somewhere to keep your thinking, that place is not built yet.

**Open Question**

- What is the unit a person sees — the note, or the claims taken out of it? The review queue chose the claim and became unusable at scale; the answer for everyday notes may be the opposite, with claims folded underneath the sentence that produced them.

**Principle**

- THE PROSE IS NEVER THE LOSER. Whatever the extractor makes of a note, the note itself is kept verbatim — fillers, comma splices and all. A system that replaced your sentence with its own reading of it would be asking you to trust a summary of your own thought.
- TRIPLES STAY THE CORE, AND FREEFORM IS THE WAY IN, NOT AN ALTERNATIVE TO THEM. Storing prose and calling it done is the easy version and it forfeits everything downstream: you cannot diff a paragraph, cite half of it, notice it contradicts another one, or record that it left a question open. Freeform capture is worth supporting because typing triples by hand is a terrible way to think — not because the triple was the problem.

**Starts With**

- A thought, in your own words, with no structure imposed on it.
