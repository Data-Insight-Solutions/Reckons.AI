# The paths are not separate products

One person moves between all five as their work changes: research until there is enough to decide, reckon to decide it, compare against a colleague who disagreed, then turn the settled result into a story for whoever comes next. The graph is the same object throughout — that continuity is the point of the whole app.

## Why it is this way

**Principle**

THE SHARING DIRECTION IS RESEARCHER -&gt; LEARNER, AND IT ONLY WORKS IF REVIEW HAPPENED. A story built on unreviewed facts teaches someone a graph nobody checked, with all the authority of a finished artifact and none of the verification. Review is not admin overhead in front of the fun part; it is what makes the sharing honest.
