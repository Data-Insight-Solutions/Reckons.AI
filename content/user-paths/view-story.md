*Concept*

> **Functional** — built and working, with rough edges still being smoothed.

## Steps

**[Start in the tangle on purpose](../user-paths/story-1)**

A good story opens on the unarranged graph.

**[Let each question pick its own layout](../user-paths/story-2)**

'How are these related' is a hierarchy question.

**[Ask Shelly about any node you land on](../user-paths/story-3)**

A story is a path through a graph, not a slideshow — you can stop and ask about anything you pass, and the answer is grounded in the graph rather than in the model's memory.

**[End on what the graph does not know](../user-paths/story-4)**

The last step of a good story is an open question, not a summary.

## Details

**Audience**

- Learners, students, anyone handed a graph by an expert. The receiving half of the sharing story.

**Ends With**

- Having understood a subject through its structure rather than through prose about it.

**Example**

- static/starter-turtles.ttl — kb:turtle-story, seven steps across six layouts. Each step asks a question the current view cannot answer, then switches to the layout that can, so layouts arrive as answers rather than as features.

**Honest Note**

- STORIES ARE AUTHORED BY HAND TODAY. There is no story editor: the steps are written as triples in the graph file. That is fine for a starter graph shipped with the app and a real barrier for a teacher who wants to build one for their class.

**Starts With**

- A graph someone else built, and no obligation to edit it.
