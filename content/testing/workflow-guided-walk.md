# Follow a guided walk through a space, step by step

A guided walk is a short tour stored inside a space. You open it, and the app shows which step you are on (step N of M), lights the items that step is about, and fades the rest. Next and Back move between steps, and every step has a link that reopens the walk there. A browser test follows a three-step tour of a small community garden, and the player's finer behavior (items never move, arrow keys, the phone layout) is checked separately by `tests/e2e/story-walk.test.ts`.

## How these screenshots were made

**What they show.** A made-up space about a community garden with a three-step walk, written by the test itself, never anyone's real notes.

**What is not shown.** The walk's optional buttons (open a page, change one setting) and the spoken narration are not part of this workflow.

## The steps

### 1. Open a walk and see step one of three

Open a story from its link and the walk starts on step one. The controls show how far along you are, and Back is switched off because there is nowhere earlier to go.

Where: the graph with the walk controls (`/?story=`).

### 2. See the items a step is about

Step one is about seedlings and beds. Those two are drawn bright and every other item is dimmed. Nothing moves; the walk only changes what stands out.

Where: the graph (`/?story=`).

**What the test checks.** Every label drawn bright is one of the step's own items, at least one is drawn, and other labels are dimmed. Where the items sit is shown by the screenshot, not measured here.

### 3. Go to the next step

Next moves to step two, and the web address now carries step 2. The brightness moves to the new step's items.

Where: the graph with the walk controls (`/?story=`).
