# Build a space from a note, then change it with another

Space building and space changing, start to finish: add a note and accept what it says in one go, then add a second note that changes some of it, and decide each change. The second half is where a space goes wrong quietly: a new value that replaces an old one has to be noticed and decided, not accepted in a batch. A browser test runs these steps, and you can run the same steps by hand with your own model (F183.3).

## How these screenshots were made

**What they show.** Both notes are invented: a community garden on an Elm Street, its beds, its coordinator and its tool shed. They are part of the notes corpus the extraction benchmark scores models on (`tests/fixtures/notes-corpus/06-garden-build.txt` and `07-garden-change.txt`).

**What is scripted.** The browser test uses no language model. Its stand-in reader returns the facts a careful reader would take from each note, written down beside the notes as the benchmark's expected answers. So the pictures show the review working on the right facts. Whether a real model reads the notes that well is what the benchmark measures, and what you see when you run the steps by hand with your own model.

## The steps

### 1. Add the first note

Open the Add tab, title the note "Garden notes" and paste: "The Elm Street community garden has twelve raised beds. Priya Shah coordinates the garden. The tool shed is open on Saturdays. Water for the beds comes from a rain barrel next to the shed." Then press Extract facts. Every fact it reads arrives waiting for your decision.

Where: the Add tab (`/ingest`).

A pink box labelled &quot;Extract facts&quot; marks the Extract facts button, under the note's title and text." loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The pink box marks the Extract facts button, under the note's title and text.</figcaption></figure>

**What the test checks.** Reading the note leaves the Add tab and puts its facts in Review, each waiting.

### 2. Accept the note's facts in one go

Open Review and choose by source. The note is one card. Nothing in it disagrees with anything yet, so its accept button offers all of it; press it once. A real model may read the note differently: refine or reject what it got wrong.

Where: the Review tab (`/review`).

<figure class="workflow-shot"><img src="/workflows/build-change/02-accept-source.webp" width="1280" height="800" alt="Screenshot of step 2, Accept the note's facts in one go. A pink box labelled &quot;Accept 4&quot; marks the note's card in Review grouped by source, and its accept button with the count it will accept." loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The pink box marks the note's card in Review grouped by source, and its accept button with the count it will accept.</figcaption></figure>

**What the test checks.** Grouped by source, the note's card offers to accept 4, and one press leaves nothing waiting.

### 3. Add a note that changes things

Back on the Add tab, title it "Garden meeting" and paste: "Notes from the garden meeting: the tool shed is now open on Sundays instead of Saturdays. Marco Ruiz is the new coordinator of the Elm Street community garden. The garden added four raised beds, so it now has sixteen." Press Extract facts.

Where: the Add tab (`/ingest`).

<figure class="workflow-shot"><img src="/workflows/build-change/03-add-change-note.webp" width="1280" height="800" alt="Screenshot of step 3, Add a note that changes things. A pink box labelled &quot;Extract facts&quot; marks the Extract facts button, with the change note filled in." loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The pink box marks the Extract facts button, with the change note filled in.</figcaption></figure>

**What the test checks.** Reading the change note leaves the Add tab and puts its facts in Review.

### 4. See what disagrees with the space

In Review, by source, the meeting note's card says how many facts are held back. Each new value sits next to the one it would replace, with the reason it is held: it disagrees with a fact you have. Accepting the card in one go would skip them; they need a decision each. The graph beside the list shows only this note's facts and what they touch.

Where: the Review tab (`/review`).

<figure class="workflow-shot"><img src="/workflows/build-change/04-see-held-back.webp" width="1280" height="800" alt="Screenshot of step 4, See what disagrees with the space. A pink box labelled &quot;Held back&quot; marks the change note's card, with its facts held back and the reason above each." loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The pink box marks the change note's card, with its facts held back and the reason above each.</figcaption></figure>

**What the test checks.** The meeting note's card shows all three facts held back as disagreeing with a fact you have, and offers no group accept.

### 5. Keep the new values

Press keep new on each one: Sunday, Marco Ruiz and 16 raised beds. The old values are kept on record as replaced, not deleted. If a real model wrote the number added instead of the new total, or the old day again, this is where you catch it.

Where: the Review tab (`/review`).

<figure class="workflow-shot"><img src="/workflows/build-change/05-keep-new.webp" width="1280" height="800" alt="Screenshot of step 5, Keep the new values. A pink box labelled &quot;Keep new&quot; marks the keep new button on a held-back fact." loading="lazy" decoding="async" /><figcaption>The pink box marks the keep new button on a held-back fact.</figcaption></figure>

**What the test checks.** Pressing keep new on each disagreement settles it until nothing is waiting.

### 6. See the space after the change

Open the graph and select the garden. Its facts are listed under the note each came from: 16 raised beds and Marco Ruiz as coordinator from the meeting, the rain barrel from the first note. The replaced values are kept on record but are no longer part of the space.

Where: the graph (`/`).

<figure class="workflow-shot"><img src="/workflows/build-change/06-see-changed-space.webp" width="1280" height="800" alt="Screenshot of step 6, See the space after the change. **What the test checks.** The space holds the new values and keeps the old ones as replaced; the garden's panel lists 16 raised beds under the meeting note and no 12.
