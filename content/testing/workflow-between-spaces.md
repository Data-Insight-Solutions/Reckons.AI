# Move between spaces: see them, follow a jump, and come back

Spaces are separate collections kept on one device. This workflow shows the whole trip: the list of your spaces, the map that draws a line between spaces that are linked, an item that carries a jump to another space, following that jump, and switching back from the list. Each space keeps its own items; a jump is just a link stored with an item (a leap, in the app's own word).

## How these screenshots were made

**What they show.** Two made-up spaces, a garden-plans space and a seed library, written by the test into its own empty browser. The tomato item in the garden space carries a jump to the seed library.

**What is not shown.** Jumps to a web address or an app page, and opening an example space from the About page, are not part of this workflow.

## The steps

### 1. See your spaces in a list

The Spaces page lists every space on this device. The one you are working in is marked as current, and the other waits below it.

Where: the Spaces page (`/kb`).
