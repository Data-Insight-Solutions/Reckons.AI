# Why Reckons.AI

Enterprise technology without the subscription. The largest data platforms on earth have converged on one way of representing meaning: the RDF triple, which is just a sentence with three parts. They sell it to governments and banks for six and seven figures a year. Reckons.AI is that same representation, running on your own machine, under an MIT licence, with no account and no invoice.

<p class="derived">It has 5 parts below.</p>

## Why it is this way

**Principle**

The pitch is democratisation rather than novelty. We did not invent the triple and we are not first to productise it: the W3C standardised RDF in 1999 and the enterprise vendors have been shipping it for two decades. What is new is that the whole stack now fits in a browser tab, which is what makes giving it away possible.
