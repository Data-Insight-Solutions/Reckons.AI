# Democratise it

Governments and banks pay millions a year to represent what they know as triples, because knowing what you know, and where each piece of it came from, turns out to be the thing that lets an organisation act. That capability has no business being restricted to organisations with a procurement department. The standards are open, the models run locally, and the storage is a file. Reckons.AI is what is left when you remove the invoice.

## Why it is this way

**Principle**

An unverifiable claim, made by the party it benefits, is not evidence. That applies to this page too, which is why every figure on it carries a source and a date.
