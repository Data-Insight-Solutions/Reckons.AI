# What the subscription actually buys

Not the triples, which are a W3C standard anyone may use. The invoice buys scale, support, governance and somebody to sue. Those are real goods, worth real money to an organisation that needs them. What they are not is a prerequisite for representing what you know as typed, sourced, answerable facts.

<p class="derived">It has one part below.</p>

## Why it is this way

**Principle**

- Every platform on this page requires an account with the vendor. Reckons.AI requires a browser. That is the whole argument, and it is an argument about who gets to participate.
- The technology is not the moat; the operations are. Once the reasoning, the storage and the models all fit on a laptop, the only thing between a person and a working knowledge graph is a purchase order, and that is a barrier we can simply remove.

## In this section

### No account, no invoice, no operator

Reckons.AI is MIT-licensed, runs in the browser against local storage, and works with no network connection. There is no login, no seat count, no usage meter, and no server we could bill you from even if we wanted to. Bring your own model: a cloud key if you want one, or a local model if you do not.

#### Why it is this way

**Honest Note**

One genuine cost is worth naming. If you point the app at a cloud model provider, you pay that provider for tokens. We take nothing, but calling it free would be a lie if we let you assume the models were free too. The local WASM and Ollama backends exist precisely so the honest answer can be zero.
