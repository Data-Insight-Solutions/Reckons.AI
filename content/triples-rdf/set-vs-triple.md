# What a set says that triples cannot

Triples alone cannot tell the difference between a fact about a GROUP and the same fact about each of its members. 'The team weighs 800 kilos' and 'each person on the team weighs 800 kilos' come out identical if the only thing you can describe is one member at a time. Giving the group its own name and its own facts separates them. That is the one thing a set genuinely adds, and it is why sets are part of the substrate rather than a way of laying out a page.

## What we found

**Example**

A set called 'DAM shortlist' can carry 'decided on 2026-08-14' and 'three of eleven considered' -- facts about the shortlist, true of no single vendor in it.
