# The Review Workflow

1. Ingest -- paste text, upload documents, import calendars. 2. Review -- the AI proposes facts and, where your text already grouped things, the groups they belong to; you confirm or reject each one. 3. Explore -- navigate your 3D knowledge graph. 4. Ask Shelly -- get answers grounded in your confirmed facts. 5. Share -- export your .ttl.
