# A set can keep your own word for membership

Most groupings already exist in your notes under a word you chose -- a project 'includes' these tasks, a menu 'offers' these dishes. Reckons.AI can mark that thing as a set without rewriting any of it, by recording which of your words carries the membership. Your vocabulary is kept; nothing is duplicated; and the group becomes something you can name, describe and view as a whole.

## What we found

**Example**

'Project Ash includes design, build, handover' stays exactly as written. One extra fact says Project Ash is a set, and another says that 'includes' is what its membership is carried by.
